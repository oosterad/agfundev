Preview only: MarbleMania is still in development and not yet available on Google Play. The game, the screens and the features on this page may still change.

A logic puzzle game on a small board that gets nasty. Roll your marbles from the top left to the bottom right, and arrive with exactly the right number of marbles.

Count before you roll

Every board tells you how many marbles you need at the finish. Pick up a +1 on the way, lose one on a −1, and make sure the numbers add up when you get there.

Marbles roll down. Mostly.

Teleporters move your marbles across the board, whirlwinds lift them one row up, gates only open with enough marbles, and walls and one-way passages block the easy route.

Make your own boards

Build your own puzzles in the editor. A built-in solver checks every board, so you know it can be solved and how short the best route is.

Share them on the World Board

Share your boards with other players, play theirs and give them stars. Sort and filter to find the most played or best rated boards.

Coming soon to Google Play

MarbleMania is not released yet. Until then, have a look at Memomania, my memory game that is already available.
