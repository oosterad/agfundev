1. Overview

MarbleMania ("the App", "we", "us") is a marble puzzle game. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and how it's handled. The App does not require creating an account with an email or password — on first start our server gives you a random ID and a secret key, which stay on your phone and identify you from then on.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what's needed to run the daily puzzle, the leaderboards, shared boards and feedback:

Your progress through the bundled puzzles, the boards you make but don't share, and your streak rewards are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us.

The App never asks for your name, email address or phone number, and has no access to your location, contacts, photos or files. There is no chat or messaging between users. The App does not send push notifications and does not send crash reports.

3. How We Use It

  • To run the daily puzzle, including your streak.
  • To show the daily top 10: your result is shown there to other players with your username.
  • To share boards between players: a shared board is visible to all players, together with your username.
  • To read and answer the feedback you send us.
  • To show ads that help keep the App free (see Section 4).

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything beyond running and improving the App.

4. Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob to help keep it free to play: a banner while you play, and an optional video ad you can choose to watch for an extra hint. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, general device information, and ad interaction data to select and measure ads.

We have configured the App to request only non-personalized, general-audience ads for all users — ads are not targeted using your browsing or app-usage history, and ad content is capped at a rating suitable for a general audience.

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Partner Sites policy and their own Privacy Policy. You can review, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's system settings.

6. Children's Privacy

MarbleMania is a general-audience game that may also appeal to children. In line with Google Play's Families Policy and children's privacy laws (including COPPA), we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age, and all ads shown in the App are non-personalized and rated for a general audience — no behavioral ad targeting is applied to any user.

If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information beyond what's described in Section 2, please contact us using the details in Section 11 and we'll address it.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your username, daily results, shared boards, ratings and feedback until you ask us to delete them. You can:

  • Stop sharing a board at any time from within the App; it is then removed from our server.
  • Ask us to delete all your data by contacting us (Section 11) with your in-app username. We then remove your username, daily results, shared boards, board progress, ratings and feedback from our server.
  • Uninstalling the App stops any further data collection from your device and removes everything the App stored on your phone, but does not delete your data from our server. Contact us for that, as described above.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (such as encrypted connections between the App and our server) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to certain processing (for example, under GDPR in the EU/UK). Since we don't collect identifying information like your email or name, we verify requests using your in-app username. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
