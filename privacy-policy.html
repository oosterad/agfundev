1. Overview

Memory Plus ("the App", "we", "us") is a memory-matching tile game. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and how it's handled. The App does not require creating an account with an email or password — you're identified only by a random ID generated on your device when you install it.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what's needed to make the App's multiplayer, friends, and notification features work:

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information. There is no in-app chat or free-text messaging between users.

3. How We Use It

  • To run the App's core gameplay, which is playable entirely solo with no data shared with other players; when you choose an optional multiplayer mode, this also covers matching you with opponents, syncing live games, and scoring async challenges.
  • To run the friends system: sending, accepting, and denying friend requests.
  • To send push notifications you've opted into (friend activity, game invites, results).
  • To show ads that help keep the App free (see Section 4).
  • To keep the App working reliably and to diagnose problems.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything beyond running and improving the App.

4. Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob to help keep it free to play. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), general device information, and ad interaction data to select and measure ads.

We have configured the App to request only non-personalized, general-audience ads for all users — ads are not targeted using your browsing or app-usage history, and ad content is capped at a rating suitable for a general audience.

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Partner Sites policy and their own Privacy Policy. Depending on your region, you may see a consent prompt on first launch that lets you manage ad-related permissions — you can also review or reset your device's ad preferences in your phone's system settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Memory is a general-audience game that may also appeal to children. In line with Google Play's Families Policy and children's privacy laws (including COPPA), we do not knowingly collect personal information such as an email address, real name, or precise location from anyone, regardless of age, and all ads shown in the App are non-personalized and rated for a general audience — no behavioral ad targeting is applied to any user.

If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information beyond what's described in Section 2, please contact us using the details in Section 11 and we'll address it.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your profile, friend, and game data for as long as your device ID remains active on the App. You can:

  • Turn off notifications at any time in the App's settings.
  • Remove friends individually from within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App stops any further data collection from your device, though your existing profile record (identified only by the random device ID, not by anything personally identifying) may remain on our server unless you request deletion.
  • Request full deletion of your data by contacting us (Section 11) with your in-app username.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (such as encrypted connections between the App and our server) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to certain processing (for example, under GDPR in the EU/UK). Since we don't collect identifying information like your email or name, we verify deletion/access requests using your in-app username. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
